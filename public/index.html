How it works

Three steps.
Two hours outside.
One great story.

We design the adventure for your neighborhood, your kids' ages, and the season. You print it and walk out the door. The phone doesn't come.

01

We build your hunt

Tell us your city and your kids' ages. We put together a custom adventure designed for your neighborhood — real landmarks, local businesses, hidden things you've walked past a hundred times.

02

Print it. Head out.

You get a beautifully simple adventure sheet. Print it, grab some water, and walk out the front door. The phone stays home — or at least in your pocket, face down.

03

Find things. Talk to people.

Each clue asks you to notice something, make something, or talk to someone. There's a debrief at the end — three questions to answer over snacks. The best conversations happen then.
