# Field Day — Project Brief

**Concept:** A platform that connects families with real-world, low-tech outdoor adventures — so they spend less time on screens and feel happier doing it.

**The paradox:** An app/website whose success metric is getting people *off* the app.

---

## Core Idea

People know they're too online. They want to do more — explore, make things, talk to strangers, be bored — but the friction of finding, committing to, and showing up for experiences is high. This platform removes that friction, then gets out of the way.

Not a content feed. Not a social graph. A structured outdoor adventure that uses the screen for 90 seconds, then sends you outside for 2–3 hours.

**Current format focus:** Scavenger hunts for families — clues that require the physical world (talking to strangers, observing nature, making things, going places).

---

## Key Questions (open)

- What does "minimal technology" mean in practice? (No app at all? SMS-only? One-tap confirm?)
- Is the value in *discovery* (find the thing) or *accountability* (actually show up)?
- Who pays — the participant, the experience provider, or a B2B wellness buyer?
- What's the wedge category to start with?
